# Group

The Human-Computer Interaction group at TU Darmstadt works on Extended Reality, Human-AI Interaction and the safety, security and ethics of immersive and AI-driven systems.

## PhD Students

- **Yanni Mei**: immersive visualization of future events (emergenCITY)
- **Jonas Wombacher**: safeguarding perceptual manipulations in AR (XR-Guard)
- **Yanni Zhang**: immersive human-agent interaction (Reasonable AI)
- **Kian Mirbagheri**: immersive deceptive interactions

**Co-supervised:** Kevin Linne (Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen), Martin Pluisch (Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg)
